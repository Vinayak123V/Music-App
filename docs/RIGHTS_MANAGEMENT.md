# Devotional Music Rights & Licensing Compliance

Bhakti Music adheres to strict music licensing standards and respect for devotional artists, acharyas, and publishers.

---

## 1. Guiding Principles

1. **No Scraping**: No audio is ever scraped from third-party streaming platforms, YouTube, or unauthorized feeds.
2. **Recorded Composition Rights**: Although traditional devotional texts (such as the *Hanuman Chalisa* composed by Goswami Tulsidas or ancient Vedic hymns) are in the public domain, specific modern musical arrangements, vocal performances, and sound recordings are protected by copyright.
3. **Mandatory Licensing Record**: Every song in Firestore contains a verified rights model:
   - `rightsHolder`
   - `licenseReference`
   - `permittedTerritories`
   - `permittedUsage`
   - `commercialAuthorized`
4. **Immediate Takedown Capability**: The Admin Console empowers administrators to unpublish any song with a single click in case of rights expiry or ownership disputes.

---

## 2. Permitted Categories of Content

- **Direct Artist / Producer Agreements**: Audio provided with explicit digital streaming rights from the artists or sound recording owners.
- **Traditional Devotional Archives & Creative Commons**: Certified CC-BY / CC-BY-SA public recordings curated with attribution.
- **In-House Studio Productions**: Bhakti Music original recordings of classical mantras and chants.

---
